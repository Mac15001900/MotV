Split a tileset into separate images: magick convert -crop 1x2@ -scene 1 input.png output_%02d.png
    Or more simply: magick input.png -crop 32x32 output.png
The reverse (combine small images into a large one): magick montage * -geometry 32x32 -background none output.png
Resize an image: magick input.png -resize 150% output.png
